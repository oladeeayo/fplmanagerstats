Why this works better

  • Dark background (#14151D) — works on both light and dark OS themes, never blends into the background
  • Bold green circle (#00FF85) — the primary brand color, instantly visible at every size
  • Pentagon + seam lines — universally recognized as a football/soccer ball, even at 16px
  • Square 512×512 viewBox — proper for PWA manifest, apple-touch-icon, and maskable icons
  • Only 6 SVG elements — tiny file size, fast to load, no complex paths
  • 10% safe zone — content stays within the maskable crop area (padded from edges)
